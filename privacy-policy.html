Paint Project Manager - Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026  |  Developer: Jeremy Massey  |  Contact: findain@gmail.com

In short: Paint Project Manager stores your data on your own device. The developer does not collect, receive, or sell your data. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no crash reporting.

1. Who we are

Paint Project Manager ("the app") is an independent paint estimating and order-workflow tool published by Jeremy Massey ("we", "us"). You can contact us at findain@gmail.com.

2. Information the app stores on your device

When you use the app, you may enter information such as project and walk details, room and surface measurements, product selections, colors, order information, and optionally customer names, phone numbers, and addresses, as well as the names and phone numbers of paint reps. If you use the quote and invoice features, you may also enter your business name, contact details, license number, terms, sales tax rate, and logo, your own product prices, production rates, and labor and supply amounts, and you may create quotes, change orders, invoices, and payment records, add photos from a walk, and capture a customer's signature on your device. This information is saved only on your device. We do not have access to it.

3. Information we do not collect

  • We do not require an account or login.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not track your location, and we do not collect device identifiers for tracking.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
  • The app does not send data to us or to any server in the background.

4. Messages you choose to send

Some features let you send a quote request or an order to a paint rep, or a "your order is ready" message to a customer. When you tap one of these buttons, the app prepares a text message and opens your device's own messaging app. Nothing is sent until you review the message and send it yourself. A message to a customer uses the customer's first name, the order number, a short count of the containers (such as 9 x 5-gal), a short job label or address when the order has one, and the phone number you already saved on that order. It never includes colors or prices. Messages to a paint rep do not include customer names, phone numbers, addresses, or prices. An order sent to a paint rep also includes a short code line at the end of the message. The code holds only the catalog, product, sheen, container size, quantity, color, an optional note, and the job label you typed, so that a copy of the app on the other end can import the order. It never holds customer names, phone numbers, addresses, or prices. Importing an order is done only when you paste a message into the app yourself; the app does not read your messages. Messages are sent from your device through your own messaging app and carrier, and your use of that service is governed by their terms and privacy policies, not this one. You decide what a message contains before you send it, and you can turn off the customer message in Settings.

5. Quotes, invoices, and other documents (PDF)

The quote, change order, invoice, and crew work order features create PDF files on your device from the information and prices you enter. A PDF is not uploaded anywhere. It leaves your device only when you choose to share it from your device's share sheet or save it to your device. These documents can contain your business information and your customer's name, phone number, and job address, so share them only with the people you intend. The prices on a document are the prices you entered, and the amounts of an invoice are the amounts you recorded; the app does not look up or supply prices. Sales tax is a percentage you set; the app only calculates it and does not provide tax advice.

A document can include payment instructions, a payment link, a deposit amount, and reply links, all of which come from what you type into the app. The app does not take, process, or see any payment: a customer who taps a payment link leaves the PDF and the app and is subject to the terms and privacy policy of that service, and you record any payment yourself. Reply links (Accept or Approve, Question or changes, Decline) are ordinary text-message and email links. When a customer taps one, it opens their own messaging or email app with a message already written to the phone number or email address you entered in your business profile. The app does not send that message and cannot receive or see the reply. A reply is a written message, not a signature, and any status you record (such as Sent, Accepted, Paid, or Overdue) is set by you and stays on your device.

You can add photos from a walk to a document, and you can let a customer sign a document on your device in person. A photo is shrunk and placed in the PDF only when you choose it. A signature is stored on your device with the customer's printed name, the date and time, and the amount agreed, and it appears only on the PDF you create. A signature captured in the app is a record of the customer's agreement kept on your device; it is not a certified electronic-signature service.

The Quotes & Invoices page and the reminder banner on the Home screen are calculated on your device from the records you keep. They do not send reminders to your customers and do not contact anyone. The CSV export is created on your device and is saved or shared only when you choose to, and it contains your customers' names, phone numbers, and addresses and your amounts, so store and share it carefully.

6. Backups and exports

The app lets you export your data to a backup file. That file is created on your device and saved where you choose. You control where it goes after that. Backup files can contain customer information, invoices, payment records, and signatures, so store and share them carefully. When you export a backup, the app lets you protect it with a passphrase. The file is then encrypted on your device with AES-256, using a key derived from your passphrase. The passphrase is not stored by the app and is never sent anywhere, so we cannot recover a forgotten passphrase or the data in that backup. A backup exported without a passphrase is not encrypted.

7. Subscriptions and payments

If the app offers a paid subscription or free trial, purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We may receive confirmation from Google Play that a subscription is active so the app can unlock its features. Google's handling of your payment information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

8. Third-party names and trademarks

Paint Project Manager is an independent product. It is not sponsored by, endorsed by, affiliated with, or associated with any retailer or paint brand referenced in the app. Brand and product names appear only as plain text to identify products, and they remain the property of their respective owners.

9. Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you control it. You can delete individual projects and orders inside the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes the data it holds on your device. Backup files you exported are separate and must be deleted by you.

10. Children

The app is intended for contractors, painters, and paint-industry professionals. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

11. Web version

If you use the web version of the app, it is served from a static website host. Like most websites, the host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, which we do not receive or use. Your project data stays in your browser's local storage on your device.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated. If we ever add a feature that collects data, we will update this policy and the app's store disclosures before that feature is released.
