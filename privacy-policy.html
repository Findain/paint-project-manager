Paint Project Manager - Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026  |  Developer: Jeremy Massey  |  Contact: findain@msn.com

In short: Paint Project Manager stores your data on your own device. The developer does not collect, receive, or sell your data. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no crash reporting.

1. Who we are

Paint Project Manager ("the app") is an independent paint estimating and order-workflow tool published by Jeremy Massey ("we", "us"). You can contact us at findain@msn.com.

2. Information the app stores on your device

When you use the app, you may enter information such as project and walk details, room and surface measurements, product selections, colors, order information, and optionally customer names, phone numbers, and addresses, as well as a paint rep's name and phone number. This information is saved only on your device. We do not have access to it.

3. Information we do not collect

  • We do not require an account or login.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not track your location, and we do not collect device identifiers for tracking.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
  • The app does not send data to us or to any server in the background.

4. Messages you choose to send

Some features let you send a quote request or an order to a paint rep. When you tap one of these buttons, the app prepares a text message and opens your device's own messaging app. Nothing is sent until you review the message and send it yourself. Messages are sent from your device through your own messaging app and carrier, and your use of that service is governed by their terms and privacy policies, not this one. You decide what a message contains before you send it.

5. Backups and exports

The app lets you export your data to a backup file. That file is created on your device and saved where you choose. You control where it goes after that. Backup files can contain customer information, so store and share them carefully. Where the app offers a passphrase for a backup, the file is encrypted with it, and we cannot recover a forgotten passphrase or the data in that backup.

6. Subscriptions and payments

If the app offers a paid subscription or free trial, purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. We may receive confirmation from Google Play that a subscription is active so the app can unlock its features. Google's handling of your payment information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Third-party names and trademarks

Paint Project Manager is an independent product. It is not sponsored by, endorsed by, affiliated with, or associated with any retailer or paint brand referenced in the app. Brand and product names appear only as plain text to identify products, and they remain the property of their respective owners.

8. Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you control it. You can delete individual projects and orders inside the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes the data it holds on your device. Backup files you exported are separate and must be deleted by you.

9. Children

The app is intended for contractors, painters, and paint-industry professionals. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Web version

If you use the web version of the app, it is served from a static website host. Like most websites, the host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, which we do not receive or use. Your project data stays in your browser's local storage on your device.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated. If we ever add a feature that collects data, we will update this policy and the app's store disclosures before that feature is released.

12. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact Jeremy Massey at findain@msn.com.
