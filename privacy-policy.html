Paint Project Manager - Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026  |  Developer: Jeremy Massey  |  Contact: findain@gmail.com

In short: Paint Project Manager stores your data on your own device. The developer does not collect, receive, or sell your data. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no crash reporting. The quotes, invoices, photos, and signatures you create stay on your device unless you choose to share or export them.

1. Who we are

Paint Project Manager ("the app") is an independent paint estimating and order-workflow tool published by Jeremy Massey ("we", "us"). You can contact us at findain@gmail.com.

2. Information the app stores on your device

When you use the app, you may enter or create the following. All of it is saved only on your device. We do not have access to it.

  • Projects and orders: project and walk details, room and surface measurements, product selections, colors, notes, order information, and optionally customer names, phone numbers, and addresses, as well as the names and phone numbers of paint reps.
  • Photos: photos you take or choose to attach to a project.
  • Your business profile: your business name, contact details, license number, logo, terms and agreement text, payment instructions, payment link, tax rate, invoice settings, and the standard scope-of-work wording you choose, such as drop cloths and taping.
  • Your pricing: your own product prices, production rates, and labor and supply amounts.
  • Documents and records: quotes (including the scope of work, any additional charges, and any discount you add), invoices, change orders, and work orders, the payments you record on an invoice (amount, date, and method), and any signature a customer draws on your device.
  • Settings: your preferences (including whether you use the app as a paint store or rep, a painting contractor, or both), counters for quote, invoice, and change order numbers, and reminder settings.

3. Information we do not collect

  • We do not require an account or login.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services.
  • We do not use cookies or send push notifications.
  • We do not track your location, and we do not collect device identifiers for tracking.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
  • The app does not send your data to us or to any server, in the background or otherwise.

4. Camera, photos, and microphone

When you add a photo to a project, the app opens your device's own camera or photo picker. Photos are saved in the app's storage on your device and are never uploaded. A photo is printed on a quote, invoice, or change order only if you choose to attach it to that document. A logo you choose for your business profile is shrunk and saved on your device in the same way. The app does not currently use the microphone.

5. Messages you choose to send

Some features let you send a quote request or an order to a paint rep, or a "your order is ready" message to a customer. When you tap one of these buttons, the app prepares a text message and opens your device's own messaging app. Nothing is sent until you review the message and send it yourself.

  • A message to a customer uses the customer's first name, the order number, a short count of the containers (such as 9 x 5-gal), a short job label or address when the order has one, and the phone number you already saved on that order. It never includes colors or prices.
  • Messages to a paint rep do not include customer names, phone numbers, addresses, or prices.
  • An order sent to a paint rep also includes a short code line at the end of the message. The code holds only the catalog, product, sheen, container size, quantity, color, an optional note, and the job label you typed, so that a copy of the app on the other end can import the order. It never holds customer names, phone numbers, addresses, or prices.
  • A payment reminder or quote follow-up to a customer (see section 8) uses the customer's first name, the invoice or quote number, the amount, the due date or a short job address, and the payment instructions you saved in your business profile. You can edit it before sending.
  • The app reads your clipboard only when you tap a Paste button, for example to import an order from a message. It does not read your messages.

Messages are sent from your device through your own messaging app and carrier, and your use of that service is governed by their terms and privacy policies, not this one. You decide what a message contains before you send it, and you can turn off the customer message in Settings.

6. Quotes, invoices, and other documents (PDF)

The Customer Quote, Invoice, Change Order, and Crew Work Order features create a PDF on your device from the prices and details you enter. The PDF is not uploaded anywhere. It leaves your device only when you choose to share it from your device's share sheet or save it to your device. A document can contain your business information and your customer's name, phone number, and job address, plus any photos you attach and any signature the customer has given, so share it only with the people you intend. A Crew Work Order is made without prices. A quote can include a scope of work: the rooms and surfaces taken from the walk, plus the prep and protection items and notes you choose. These come from your walk and from text you enter, and print only on documents you create. A quote's sales tax settings and production rates are copied onto the quote on your device when you first send it, so later changes to your settings do not alter it.

The prices and amounts on a document are the ones you entered. The app does not look up or supply prices. If you enter a sales tax percentage, the app only calculates the amount from the number you typed. It does not look up tax rates and does not give tax, legal, or accounting advice. Hours estimated from your production rates are a calculation from your own numbers.

A document can include payment instructions, a payment link, and a deposit amount, all of which come from what you type into the app. The app does not take, process, or see any payment. A customer who taps a payment link leaves the PDF and the app and is subject to the terms and privacy policy of that service. You record payments yourself on an invoice, and the status of a quote or invoice (Sent, Accepted, Paid, and so on) is set by you. The app does not know whether an invoice has been paid.

A quote can also include reply links (Accept, Question or changes, Decline). These are ordinary text-message and email links. When a customer taps one, it opens their own messaging or email app with a message already written to the phone number or email address you entered in your business profile. The app does not send that message and cannot receive or see the reply. A reply is a written message, not a signature.

7. Customer signatures

If you hand your device to a customer to sign a quote, invoice, or change order, the app saves the drawn signature as a small image on your device, together with the printed name the customer typed, the date and time, and the agreement text and total they approved. It is not uploaded anywhere. It prints on a document, and is included in a backup, only when you create that document or backup. A signature saved in the app is a record of the customer's agreement on your device. It is not a certified e-signature service. If a quote's amounts change after it was signed, the app no longer prints the old signature on it.

8. Reminders, summaries, and spreadsheet export

The Quotes & Invoices screen and the reminders in the app are worked out on your device from the records you keep, such as unpaid invoices, invoices due soon or overdue, and quotes waiting on a reply. They appear inside the app when you open it. The app does not send notifications, emails, or texts to you, to your customers, or to us. The Quotes & Invoices screen can offer a Text reminder on an invoice that is due soon or overdue, and a Text follow-up on a quote that is waiting on a reply. Tapping one opens your device's own messaging app with a message already written to the phone number saved on the walk. Nothing is sent until you review it and press Send yourself. The app keeps only the date and time you tell it you sent the message, so it can show a note such as "Reminded Nov 5", and that note stays on your device. You can create a CSV file of your quotes, invoices, payments, and change orders for your bookkeeper. The file is created on your device, and it can include customer names, phone numbers, and addresses. It is saved or shared only where you choose.

9. Backups and exports

The app lets you export your data to a backup file. That file is created on your device and saved where you choose. A complete backup can include everything described in section 2, and a backup made with photos also includes your photos. You control where the file goes after that. Backup files can contain customer information and signatures, so store and share them carefully. When you export a backup, the app lets you protect it with a passphrase. The file is then encrypted on your device with AES-256, using a key derived from your passphrase. The passphrase is not stored by the app and is never sent anywhere, so we cannot recover a forgotten passphrase or the data in that backup. A backup exported without a passphrase is not encrypted.

10. Internet use and the web version

The app works offline. When it is online, it may check for a newer version of itself by requesting its own files from the website it was installed from. That request does not include your project data. If you use the web version, it is served from a static website host. Like most websites, the host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, which we do not receive or use. Your project data stays in your device's local storage and is never sent to the host. Links you tap in a document, such as a payment link, open in your browser or another app and are governed by that service's own policies.

11. Subscriptions and payments

If the app offers a paid subscription or free trial, purchases are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details. In the Android app, the app asks Google Play, on your device, whether your subscription or free trial is active, and keeps that answer on your device so it can work offline. That answer is not included in backups. If your subscription or trial ends, you can still open, edit and finish your existing projects and back up your data, but you cannot create new projects, Quick Orders or reorders until you subscribe again. The web version does not use Google Play billing. Google's handling of your payment information is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. This is separate from the payments your own customers make to you, which the app does not handle.

It is not sponsored by, endorsed by, affiliated with, or associated with any retailer or paint brand referenced in the app. Brand and product names appear only as plain text to identify products, and they remain the property of their respective owners.

13. Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you control it. You can delete individual projects, orders, invoices, and change orders inside the app. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes the data it holds on your device, including photos and signatures. Backup files, spreadsheet exports, and documents you saved or shared are separate and must be deleted by you or by the people you sent them to.

14. Your customers' information

You may enter your customers' names, phone numbers, addresses, photos, and signatures into the app. You are responsible for using that information lawfully, for keeping your device and backups secure, and for any notices to your customers that the law requires of you. We do not receive this information.

15. Children

The app is intended for contractors, painters, and paint-industry professionals. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

16. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date at the top shows when it was last updated. If we ever add a feature that collects data or uses the microphone, we will update this policy and the app's store disclosures before that feature is released.
